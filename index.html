About this tracker

What's on the board

Assets with a documented, public link to Donald Trump or the Trump family — coins he personally launched and promoted, the company he holds a large stake in, family-backed crypto ventures, and assets he's publicly endorsed.

How "live" works

Crypto prices refresh straight from the public CoinGecko API every time you load the page (and on a timer). Stocks don't have a free, key-less live feed that works in the browser, so each stock links out to a live quote instead of showing a number that could go stale.

What this is NOT

This is not a feed of secret "buy now" tips, and it does not claim Trump told anyone to buy anything. It's an informational dashboard of public connections. See the disclaimer below.
