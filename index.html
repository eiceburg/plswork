About this tracker

What's on the board

Stocks tied to Donald Trump by public financial disclosures (companies he's recently bought) and his own public statements (companies he's praised or told people to buy). Each entry links to the news source.

How "live" works

Prices, today's % change and the mini graphs are pulled live from a public market-data feed every time you load the page and on a 60-second timer. If the feed can't be reached, the board still renders — just without live numbers.

What this is NOT

This is not a "buy now" tip sheet and not a claim that Trump told you to buy anything. Reporting notes these moves have raised conflict-of-interest questions; his team says advisers manage the holdings. See the disclaimer.
